DriveBeats

Generative music that follows how you drive.

Before you start

  • Phone rigidly mounted, screen up, top edge forward, tilted slightly nose-down.
  • Turn off the silent switch — iOS will mute the audio otherwise.
  • Plugged in. Do not touch the phone while moving.

If it reacts backwards

  • The iOS axis signs are not confirmed yet. If braking sounds like accelerating, tap Flip forward once — it is remembered.

No motion events received — motion access may have been denied.
